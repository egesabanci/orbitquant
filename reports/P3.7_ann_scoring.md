# P3.7 — ANN-Specific TurboQuant Scoring Path

**Status:** Implemented and benchmarked (pure NumPy, no model/GPU).
**Files:** `baseline/p37_ann.py`, `baseline/run_p37.py`
**Run:** `python3 -m baseline.run_p37 --d 64 --nrot 2000`

## Purpose

For nearest-neighbor search, score directly over TurboQuant codes and rerank
only a small candidate set in full precision — avoiding full dequantization
during search. TurboQuant already reports strong recall and near-zero indexing
time; the next systems win is not dequantizing the whole corpus per query.

## Method

Synthetic dataset: 5000 database unit vectors, 100 queries, b=2 bits/coord
(exact-Beta Lloyd-Max, 4 centroids). Independent Haar rotation per trial. Two
scoring paths:
- **full:** top-k straight off scores from dequantizing the ENTIRE code corpus
  (the systems path P3.7 replaces);
- **score+rerank:** top-C (C = cand_scale·k, cand_scale=8) by a genuine blockwise
  ADC code-index scan over the stored codes (per-block centroid lookup, never
  materializes the corpus), then exact inner products against the original
  vectors for the C candidates only (reads back C columns of X).

Recall@k measured against exact ground truth over 2000 trials.

## Results — d=64, nrot=2000, b=2, n_db=5000, n_q=100, seed=0

```
   k     C  full_dequant  score+rerank     gain
   1     8        0.4037        0.8603   0.4566
   5    40        0.5141        0.9534   0.4393
  10    80        0.5498        0.9715   0.4217
```

## Findings

1. **Score+rerank massively beats scoring over the dequantized corpus**:
   recall@1 0.86 vs 0.40, recall@10 0.97 vs 0.55. The rerank path reads back
   only C=8k original vectors (≤80 at k=10) but recovers the true top-k that
   the noisy 2-bit scores rank poorly.

2. **Why:** the top-k of noisy approximate scores is unreliable, but the true
   top-k almost always falls within the top-C of the approximate scores. Exact
   rerank of that small candidate set restores recall. The dequantized-corpus
   baseline dequantizes all 5000 vectors but still scores with 2-bit error —
   dequantization alone does not fix the ranking noise.

3. **The gain is stable across k** (~0.42-0.46 recall points) and the ADC scan
   is a genuine code-index scan (verified numerically identical to the
   dequantized baseline, diff 0.0), so the comparison is apples-to-apples on
   the quantized representation.

## Implications

- ANN scoring over TurboQuant codes + small-set rerank (P3.7) is validated and
  strongly positive: ~2x recall@1 vs the dequantize-everything baseline, with
  far less dequantization work (only C original vectors read back).
- This is the systems win the proposal predicted: avoid full dequantization
  during search; score over codes and rerank a small candidate set.
- Combines with P3.3 (query batching) and P2.1 (fused kernels) on the GPU box:
  the ADC scan is kernel-friendly, and the rerank step is a small dense pass.
- The statistical harness validates the ranking property; real embedding
  distributions (which have structure the random-sphere model lacks) should be
  confirmed on a real vector database later.
